Choose a Subfield and Join the Hunt

Explore human behavior, society, politics, and culture through the eyes of a problem-hunter.

  • 🏛️ Create Your Own Political Party
    Design a political party that could thrive in today’s global or national landscape. Create the party’s platform, values, and key policies on major issues such as climate change, economy, healthcare, or education. Define your party’s identity—what sets it apart from existing political parties? Create campaign slogans, a party logo, and a social media strategy.
  • 🌍 Political Debate Simulation
    Choose a global political issue (e.g., climate change policy, international trade, human rights, or global health) and design a structured political debate between two opposing sides. Each team will argue their side with well-researched arguments, public speaking techniques, and effective use of rhetoric. Afterward, the audience votes on which side presented the most convincing arguments.
  • 🗳️ Design Your Own Electoral System
    Design a unique electoral system for a fictional country. This could be a modification of an existing system (e.g., proportional representation, ranked-choice voting) or a completely new approach. Consider factors like voter inclusivity, fairness, and how it might impact political outcomes. Present your electoral system, and explain how it would improve or challenge current political dynamics.
  • 🌍 International Treaty Negotiation
    Choose a global issue (e.g., climate change, cybersecurity, international trade, or refugee rights) and simulate a negotiation between diplomats from different countries. Each team represents a different nation, and together, you will craft a treaty that addresses the issue. After the negotiation, the teams present the treaty’s main provisions and argue why it is beneficial to all parties involved.
  • 🏛️ Political Cartoon Creation
    Create a political cartoon that satirizes or critiques a current political issue (e.g., corruption, political polarization, social justice movements). The cartoon should effectively use humor, symbolism, and visual language to communicate a message. Present your cartoon and explain the political issue it addresses and how the visual elements enhance the message.
  • 🌍 Policy Think Tank
    Choose a major political issue (e.g., universal healthcare, economic inequality, climate change, education reform) and develop a comprehensive policy proposal. Your proposal should include specific strategies, goals, and metrics for success. Consider the political, economic, and social implications of your policy. Present your policy proposal as if you are presenting it to a governmental body or policy think tank.
  • 🗳️ Political Movie Script
    Write a scene for a political thriller movie that draws inspiration from a current political scandal, power struggle, or geopolitical tension. The scene should have intense dialogue, political intrigue, and a dramatic climax. Perform the scene, and explain how it draws from real political events while enhancing the drama for cinematic effect.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Create a New Social Institution
    Design a new social institution (e.g., a community-based organization, educational program, or support network) that addresses a current social issue like homelessness, mental health, racial inequality, or climate change. Create the structure, goals, and methods of this institution. Explain how it would function within society and what impact it would have on the community.
  • 🌍 Sociological Experiment Design
    Choose a sociological theory (e.g., social stratification, group behavior, deviance) and design an experiment to test one of its predictions. Your experiment could involve fieldwork, surveys, or participant observation. Explain your research design, the variables you're testing, and the potential implications of your findings.
  • 🤝 Create a Social Movement
    Create a new social movement that advocates for a contemporary social cause (e.g., environmental justice, gender equality, racial reconciliation). Develop the movement’s goals, methods of protest, outreach strategies, and key messages. Explain how your movement would mobilize people, raise awareness, and influence social change.
  • 🏙️ Design a Utopian Society
    Design a utopian society that solves current social issues like inequality, poverty, crime, or environmental destruction. Define the social, economic, and political structures that would exist in this society. How would this society address issues like education, healthcare, or employment? Present your vision of this utopia and explain how it differs from the current social systems.
  • 👥 Sociological Fiction
    Write and perform a short fictional story that explores a contemporary sociological issue (e.g., social stratification, cultural assimilation, family dynamics, or social media influence). Create characters that reflect diverse social backgrounds and illustrate how they interact with the issue. Perform the story as a dramatic reading, capturing the emotional and social complexities of the narrative.
  • 🏙️ Urban Planning for Social Equity
    Design a city neighborhood that promotes social equity, inclusivity, and sustainability. Focus on addressing issues like affordable housing, access to education, transportation, and community spaces. Ensure that your design takes into account the needs of marginalized or vulnerable groups. Present your design and explain how it creates a more equitable and inclusive urban environment.
  • 🌱 Social Policy Proposal
    Choose a contemporary social issue (e.g., healthcare inequality, unemployment, child welfare, racial justice) and develop a detailed social policy proposal to address the issue. Your policy should include specific goals, strategies for implementation, and measurable outcomes. Present the policy to a group of “lawmakers” (classmates) and argue for its adoption.
  • 🧠 Design a Therapy App
    Create a digital mental health therapy app that addresses a specific psychological issue, such as anxiety, depression, or stress management. Include self-help tools, guided meditations, progress tracking, or mood analytics. Explain evidence-based strategies aligned with psychological theories.
  • 🧠 Psychological Experiment Reimagined
    Design a new psychological experiment to test or extend a classic theory (e.g., Pavlov’s conditioning, Maslow’s hierarchy). Propose methods, variables, hypothesis, and expected outcomes.
  • 🌱 Create a New Psychological Disorder
    Invent a new psychological disorder with symptoms and diagnostic criteria. Design a treatment plan using evidence-based interventions such as CBT or mindfulness. Explain diagnosis and treatment in clinical settings.
  • 🧠 Psychological Movie Script
    Write and perform a scene from a psychological thriller or drama based on a psychological concept (e.g., dissociative identity disorder, cognitive dissonance). Create compelling characters and plot with psychological tension.
  • 🧠 Psychology of Persuasion Campaign
    Create a persuasive ad campaign using psychological principles like reciprocity or social proof. Design ads, slogans, and strategies that influence consumer behavior. Explain why strategies are effective.
  • 🧑‍⚕️ Psychological Support Group
    Design a support group program for a specific population (e.g., trauma survivors, teenagers with anxiety). Outline structure, meeting topics, and therapeutic activities to improve mental well-being.
  • 🧠 Create a Psychological Theory
    Develop a new psychological theory explaining a common human behavior or experience (e.g., procrastination, social bonding). Outline core principles, real-life application, and research support possibilities.
  • ⚖️ Design a Crime Prevention Program
    Create a community-based crime prevention program targeting a specific crime type (e.g., gang violence, cybercrime). Include deterrence, rehabilitation, and community engagement strategies. Use criminological theories like social disorganization or strain theory.
  • 👮‍♂️ Reinvent the Criminal Justice System
    Propose reforms for the criminal justice system (e.g., prison overcrowding, juvenile justice) based on criminological research. Discuss how to improve fairness, reduce recidivism, and address systemic inequalities with practical solutions.
  • 🕵️‍♂️ Design a Criminal Profiling System
    Create a profiling system to identify patterns in serial crimes (e.g., murder, arson). Use criminological and psychological theories like FBI profiling. Explain how it assists real-world investigations.
  • 👨‍⚖️ Create a Crime Drama Episode
    Write and perform a crime drama episode incorporating theories like social control or labeling theory. The plot should involve crime-solving and exploring criminal motives. Showcase criminological theory in character decisions.
  • 🏛️ Design a Rehabilitation Program for Offenders
    Develop a rehabilitation program for offenders (e.g., drug or juvenile offenders). Include psychological, educational, and social interventions to reduce re-offending. Explain how your program addresses root causes based on theory.
  • ⚖️ Create a Social Media Awareness Campaign
    Design a campaign to raise awareness of a crime issue (e.g., human trafficking, cyberbullying). Use engaging media like videos or infographics. Consider criminological research on social impact and media influence.
  • 🔍 Design a Crime Data Analytics Tool
    Build a tool using crime stats, social factors, and criminological theories to predict crime hotspots. Use data visualization to show trends and assist law enforcement resource allocation.
  • 🏫 Design a Virtual Classroom
    Create a virtual classroom that enhances engagement and learning. Include interactive content, gamification, personalized learning, and collaboration. Apply educational theories like constructivism or social learning.
  • 📚 Create a Curriculum for the Future
    Design a curriculum preparing students for 21st-century challenges. Integrate critical thinking, digital literacy, emotional intelligence, and global awareness to equip learners for a changing world.
  • 👩‍🏫 Develop an Inclusive Classroom Activity
    Design an activity adaptable for students with diverse needs. Use differentiated instruction, Universal Design for Learning, and accessible materials to ensure participation and success for all.
  • 🌱 Create a Teacher Training Program
    Develop training for educators on modern pedagogy, including technology integration, differentiated instruction, and culturally responsive teaching to foster inclusive, student-centered classrooms.
  • 🧠 Design a Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Program
    Create an SEL program supporting student mental health and interpersonal skills. Include lessons on self-awareness, empathy, and relationship-building to promote a positive school climate.
  • 💡 Create an Educational Game
    Design an educational game teaching a subject or concept engagingly. Use game mechanics like levels, rewards, and challenges to motivate active learning and mastery.
  • 📝 Reimagine the Homework Experience
    Design a new homework approach that motivates students and improves outcomes. Consider workload, well-being, collaboration, and tech integration to make homework meaningful and less stressful.
