Pick a Subfield and Begin the Hunt

From startups to strategy, business is your battlefield. Think bold. Lead wild.

  • 🚀 Startup Pitch Perfect
    Develop a full business plan for a new venture, then pitch it to a mock investor panel. Convince them with your market analysis, product vision, revenue model, and growth plan.
  • 🛠️ Prototype Prodigy
    Create a product prototype (digital or physical) to solve a real-world problem. Use sketches, mock-ups, wireframes, or 3D models to show off your innovation.
  • 📈 Market Disruption Game
    Invent a business model that shakes up an entire industry. Explain how your idea reduces cost, improves access, or changes the game for consumers and competitors.
  • 💸 Financial Wizard
    Your startup needs funding. Build a strategy to raise capital from VCs, angels, or crowdfunding. Explain your ask, how it’s spent, and how you’ll deliver ROI.
  • 🌍 Global Expansion Visionary
    Take your startup international. Choose a target market and develop a plan that handles cultural, legal, logistical, and marketing challenges for global success.
  • 🏆 Customer Acquisition Quest
    Design a marketing strategy to attract and retain customers. Use online tactics, word-of-mouth, partnerships, and service excellence to build a loyal customer base.
  • 📊 Pivot or Persevere?
    Your original business plan hit a wall. Based on new market feedback, design a pivot strategy that adapts your product, customer segment, or model, and gives your startup a second wind.
  • 🛍️ Brand Rebirth
    You’re the new CMO for a failing brand. Design a bold marketing campaign that revives public interest and rebuilds trust. Use storytelling, platform strategy, and fresh messaging to bring the brand back to life.
  • 📱 Influencer Marketing Master
    Choose a product and build a campaign powered by influencers. Select creators, plan their content, choose platforms, and define the success metrics that will make your campaign stand out.
  • 🎯 Target Market Wizard
    Break a new market into clear segments and customize messages for each one. Use psychographics, behaviors, and media preferences to craft a high-impact product launch strategy.
  • 💡 Viral Campaign Architect
    Create a campaign designed to explode on social media. Use humor, emotion, or surprise to inspire sharing. Choose your format and map out the viral growth mechanics.
  • 🏆 Product Launch Extravaganza
    Craft a pre-launch, launch, and post-launch marketing plan. Include teaser content, live events, influencer activations, and follow-up strategies to maximize attention and sales.
  • 📊 Data-Driven Marketing
    Build a campaign powered by customer analytics. Use purchase history, behavior tracking, and segmentation to send tailored content and offers that drive conversions.
  • 🌍 Global Expansion Strategy
    You’re expanding into a new country. Design a localized marketing strategy that respects cultural nuances, follows regulations, and hits home with your new target audience.
  • 💼 Create Your Own Investment Fund
    Launch an innovative investment fund focused on growth, sustainability, and risk. Build a portfolio, justify your asset choices, and pitch it to future investors.
  • 📊 The Financial Crisis Reversal
    Your fictional economy has crashed. Draft a full stabilization plan using real-world monetary, fiscal, and regulatory policy tools to recover trust and growth.
  • 💡 Financial Forecasting for the Future
    Predict the next decade of market behavior using macro trends, data analysis, and a forecasting model. Show how your model supports long-term investment decisions.
  • 🏦 Cryptocurrency for the Masses
    Design a secure, energy-efficient cryptocurrency ecosystem that solves real adoption problems. Define its blockchain architecture, mining model, and global use case.
  • 📉 The Ultimate Budgeting Challenge
    Create a detailed personal or business budget that optimizes for savings and sustainability. Identify expenses to trim, investments to grow, and habits to form.
  • 📈 The Stock Pitch Showdown
    Pitch a stock that you believe will outperform over the next 3–5 years. Justify your pick with market trends, financial data, and a compelling narrative.
  • 💳 Financial Risk Management for a Global Corporation
    You're designing a multinational risk strategy: currency swings, geopolitics, and market volatility. Propose tools like hedging, insurance, and diversification tactics.
  • 💡 The Policy Maker
    Design a national economic policy to tackle a major issue like inflation, unemployment, or climate change. Justify your decisions with data, theory, and predicted outcomes.
  • 📊 Market Dynamics Master
    Identify a market failure and propose an economically sound solution, like a subsidy, tax, or regulation, to correct it. Use key concepts like elasticity and efficiency in your argument.
  • 🌍 Global Trade Strategist
    Develop a trade policy for a specific country. Design tariffs, agreements, or export plans based on its economic strengths and global role. Analyze potential trade-offs.
  • 💰 The Economist’s Forecast
    Create a macroeconomic forecast for the next decade using trends in GDP, inflation, and tech innovation. Justify your projections with models and present them to a mock advisory board.
  • 🏛️ The Fiscal Planner
    Design a balanced national budget. Decide how much to spend and where, education, defense, healthcare, and how to generate revenue. Your goal: growth with minimal deficit.
  • 📉 The Supply Chain Economist
    Create a plan to minimize the economic impact of supply chain disruptions. Use tools like cost-benefit analysis and elasticity to build resilience and restore flow.
  • 🔄 Behavioral Economist
    Use behavioral economics to design a “nudge” that changes consumer or worker behavior. From boosting savings to cutting energy use, make it smart, subtle, and impactful.
  • 🏢 The Crisis Manager
    Your company’s image is on the line. Design a crisis management plan to handle a PR disaster, restore public trust, and guide internal teams through the storm.
  • 🧑‍💼 Leadership Styles Showdown
    Develop a training program to grow future leaders in your company. Combine various leadership styles, decision-making techniques, and people-first strategies into one powerful workshop.
  • 📊 Performance Metrics Master
    Create a comprehensive performance management system that helps a fast-scaling business align its teams with strategic goals while keeping motivation high and evaluations fair.
  • 🌍 The Global Expansion Planner
    Your company is going global. Design a strategy for entering a new international market, addressing cultural fit, legal hurdles, and competition, all while building local credibility.
  • 📈 Change Management Challenge
    Merging, transforming, or adopting new tech? Design a change management plan that gets buy-in from employees, minimizes resistance, and keeps operations moving smoothly.
  • 🤝 Team Synergy Builder
    Build a program that strengthens collaboration, problem-solving, and trust across departments. Include icebreakers, role-switching activities, and feedback loops that bring out the best in teams.
  • 📋 Strategic Goal Setter
    Design a system of SMART goals that cascades through the organization, helping every team and individual stay aligned with long-term vision, and measure their progress clearly.
  • 📊 Data-Driven Decision Maker
    Analyze business data to uncover trends and create a strategy that improves sales, customer satisfaction, and marketing ROI. Back your plan with stats, models, and visuals.
  • 🔍 Market Segmentation Sleuth
    Use clustering algorithms or segmentation methods on customer data to group your audience. Then, tailor marketing strategies to each segment to boost results.
  • 🔄 Predictive Modeling Master
    Build a predictive model using historical data to forecast sales, customer churn, or behavior. Test its accuracy and explain how your model supports strategic decisions.
  • 🏆 Dashboard Designer
    Create an interactive dashboard that tracks KPIs and reveals performance insights in real-time. Use tools like Tableau, Power BI, or Excel to bring your dashboard to life.
  • 📉 Anomaly Detection Detective
    Detect unusual patterns in business operations. Use data analytics to uncover fraud, process inefficiencies, or system errors hiding in plain sight.
  • 🌍 Global Business Intelligence
    Plan an international expansion strategy using real-world data on demographics, trends, and economics. Use analytics to identify ideal markets and entry tactics.
  • ⚖️ Optimization Overload
    Your supply chain is lagging. Use data and optimization tools to streamline delivery, reduce costs, and maximize customer satisfaction with a smarter logistics strategy.
