Pick Your Discipline and Start the Hunt

Whether you're decoding DNA, building machines, or crunching numbers, each subfield hides a challenge only the bold can conquer.

  • 🧠 AI Therapist Simulator
    Create an empathetic chatbot for students facing wild exam-week problems. Can it handle stress... and sarcasm?
  • 👾 Bug Battle Royale
    Invent your own programming language to conquer coding annoyances, whitespace warlords, semicolon soldiers, and more.
  • 🌍 Save the World with Code
    Build a hilarious app to fix major “problems” like sock loss, “k.” texters, or password amnesia.
  • 🎮 Boss Fight Engine
    Design a dramatic 2D boss battle engine for absurd enemies like “The Professor Who Won’t Curve.”
  • 🤖 Dysfunctional Robot Olympics
    Build a buggy robot racer: one that avoids the color blue or stops to ponder the void. First to the finish (somehow) wins.
  • 📦 The Overengineer’s Dilemma
    Print “Hello, World” using AI, blockchain, Morse code, and at least one unnecessary microservice. Go!
  • 🔒 Escape the Virtual Room (With Code!)
    Create a digital escape room where players must debug cursed code and escape "The Developer’s Dungeon."
  • ⚙️ Frankenstein’s Toaster
    Build a single device that toasts, blends, and brews, all using salvaged mechanical parts. Viva la breakfast chaos!
  • 🛠️ The Ultimate Office Chair
    Prototype a hyper-ergonomic throne for marathon coding. Think massage features, posture sensors, and maybe a built-in scream pillow.
  • 🚗 Build-a-Car: Apocalypse Edition
    Design a rugged survival vehicle that runs on scraps, crosses wastelands, and (ideally) looks cooler than Mad Max's ride.
  • 🧼 Soapbox Derby of Doom
    Gravity-powered car + payload + stunt finale = mechanical madness. Bonus points for watermelon survival and dramatic flair.
  • 🔥 Thermodynamic Cook-Off
    Cook a meal using solar, chemical, or scavenged heat, no fire, no batteries, just your thermodynamic ingenuity.
  • 🎢 Tiny Theme Park Tycoon
    Create a tabletop amusement ride with rotating, launching, and surprise mechanics, all in under 2 square feet.
  • 📦 The Great Rube Goldberg Gauntlet
    Complete a mundane task like watering a plant with an outrageous contraption: pulleys, springs, levers, and squeaky toys encouraged.
  • 🧬 Build-a-Cell Battle Arena
    Design a fictional cell with new organelles and absurd powers, then enter it into the molecular combat league known as the Cell Games.
  • 🌱 Mutant Plant Olympics
    Engineer a plant that thrives on a planet with lava lakes, TikTok noise, or constant darkness. Adaptation is everything!
  • 🧫 Microscopic Crime Scene
    A bacterial colony is dead. Solve the mystery using PCR, gel electrophoresis, and other molecular clues from the crime scene.
  • 🧠 Brainstorm: Make a New Nervous System
    Build a hypothetical nervous system for a creature with 9 legs, no eyes, and vibration-based communication.
  • 🐍 Evolutionary Mash-Up
    Create an evolutionary timeline where a bird breathes underwater and uses echolocation. Explain how nature got weird.
  • 🍔 Digest-O-Matic 9000
    Your alien eats memes and battery acid. Design its digestive system: enzymes, organs, adaptations, go wild.
  • 💉 Vaccine Jam!
    Design a vaccine for the imaginary virus “Procrastinitus-23”, symptoms include sudden naps and snack hoarding. Explain how it works.
  • ⚡ The Great Energy Heist
    Fool the physics community with a convincingly fake perpetual motion machine. Use made-up formulas, pseudo-science, and unshakable confidence.
  • 🕳️ Build-a-Black Hole (at Home!)
    Simulate black holes using marbles, balloons, or fabric. Explain gravity wells, event horizons, and maybe even spaghettification... safely.
  • 🧲 Magneto’s Internship Challenge
    Build an overly magnetic device to solve daily problems like folding laundry or swiping on your phone, so Magneto might hire you.
  • 🚀 DIY Rocket Race: The Soda Wars
    Use soda bottles, Mentos, and Newtonian physics to win a ridiculous backyard rocket war. Calculate range, thrust, and splash radius.
  • 🔭 Time Traveler’s Tech Lab
    Pitch a theoretically plausible time machine based on relativity or wormholes. Bonus points for startup buzzwords and fake funding slides.
  • 🎢 Theme Park of Forces
    Design a theme park ride for PhysiLand that teaches inertia, gravity, friction, and acceleration, and still makes people scream.
  • 🧪 Quantum Tinder
    Build a dating app where matches exist in superposition until swiped. Use entanglement to explain long-distance love. It’s physics meets feelings.
  • ⚗️ Elemental Chef: Periodic Table Bake-Off
    Invent a fictional edible compound using real chemical bonds and elements. Explain taste, bonding, and whether it might accidentally explode.
  • ☣️ Villain Lab: Design a Designer Toxin
    Create a funny, non-lethal toxin with real chemistry behind it, blue sweat? Helium voice? You’re the mastermind of mild chaos.
  • 🧪 Build-a-Bond Battle
    Design a molecule for superhero gear. Flame-resistant? Stretchy? Camouflaged in water? Pick your monomers and defend your polymer.
  • 🧫 Petri-Dish Pop Star
    Engineer a glowing microorganism for a concert. Choose your genes, tune the emission, and explain the chemiluminescent effect.
  • 🔥 Explodium: The Safest Dangerous Compound Ever
    Create a fizzing, popping, mock explosive for movie props, dramatic but totally safe. Describe the fun reaction mechanisms.
  • 🧊 Periodic Table Pokémon
    Design a creature based on a weird element, synthetic, radioactive, or noble. Its powers must reflect the element's real chemistry.
  • 💧 Water You Doing?!
    Invent a plausible alternative to water for alien life. Justify its solvency, thermal stability, and compatibility with biochemistry.
  • 🧮 Math Island: The Function Survivor
    Invent a function with wild but consistent behavior. Maybe it's periodic only on prime inputs, or its outputs “rhyme.” Bonus points for a weird graph.
  • 📊 Statistical Conspiracy
    Create a fake data set to statistically “prove” something absurd, like pizza boosts grades. Use real methods, bias, regression, confidence intervals, the works.
  • 🔢 The Algorithm Gauntlet
    Invent your own sorting algorithm… with a personality. It might procrastinate, ignore half the inputs, or “sort by mood” — but it has to work eventually.
  • 🧠 Proof Chef: Bake Me a Theorem
    Create a new mathematical identity and attempt a proof. Maybe it’s beautiful, maybe it’s cursed. Bonus points if it’s both.
  • 🔺 Geometry Gladiators: Shape Evolution
    Invent a new shape that solves a real problem, packing, building, decorating, and define its area, volume, angles, and purpose.
  • ⏳ Time-Traveling Mathematician
    Model time travel using imaginary numbers, set theory, or logic graphs. Your mission: resolve paradoxes with math. Bonus for diagrams that melt brains.
  • 💰 Crypto Chaos
    Design a new cryptosystem using chaos theory, modular arithmetic, or graph theory. Make it secure enough to baffle your math teacher (or aliens).
  • ⚡ The Voltage Villain Showdown
    Build a circuit trap to catch a cartoon villain stealing donuts, using sensors, logic gates, and a dramatic buzzer or LED alert.
  • 🔋 Battery Royale
    Keep an appliance running with the weirdest power combo you can build: lemon batteries? solar cranks? You must prove it works (mostly).
  • 🤖 Robot Karaoke Judge
    Create a sound-reactive circuit that listens to singers and responds with lights, voice clips, or mock applause. It judges. Hard.
  • 📡 Wi-Fight Club
    Build a short-range, secret wireless communication system, using light, RF, or IR, and smuggle coded messages across enemy lines.
  • 💡 LED Fashion Week
    Make wearables that blink, pulse, or flash based on sound, motion, or puns. High voltage haute couture!
  • 🎮 Retro Arcade Hackathon
    Build a basic game using circuits: reaction tests, LED races, or sound puzzles. Use 555 timers, counters, and old-school hardware charm.
  • 🔌 The Great Circuit Circus
    Light an LED only after the most absurd chain of electrical events, potato batteries, IR triggers, motion sensors, and dramatic delays welcome.
  • 📊 The Imaginary Forecast
    Build a model to predict the winner of an imaginary global event using made-up but structured data. Justify your variables and logic.
  • 🧠 AI as a Superpower
    Design an AI system to solve a real-world global issue, like climate change, pandemics, or poverty. Pick your model and explain how it works.
  • 📈 The Data Strategy Game
    Act as chief data scientist for a fictional startup. Create a data collection and analysis strategy that drives smart decision-making.
  • 🌍 The Mystery Data
    Predict the outcome of a fictional city-wide change (e.g., all buses go electric). Use logic and common sense, not numbers, to guide your predictions.
  • 🔄 Data-Driven Decision-Making in Crisis
    After a natural disaster, design a real-time decision framework using population, weather, and resource data to coordinate emergency response.
  • 🔍 Algorithm for Good
    Invent an algorithm that fights fake news, reduces AI bias, or flags discrimination. Outline the logic and justify its ethical impact.
  • 🎮 Data Science Game Show: What’s Your Hypothesis?
    Propose a testable hypothesis (e.g., “Sleep boosts GPA”) and design an experiment. Discuss how you’d collect data and control for bias and noise.
  • 🏗️ The Ultimate Bridge Challenge
    Design a conceptual bridge that can survive both earthquakes and floods. Detail materials, shock-absorbing design, and your disaster-proofing techniques.
  • 🏢 Eco-Friendly Skyscraper
    Design a futuristic skyscraper powered by renewable energy, built with sustainable materials, and optimized for urban living challenges.
  • 🌍 Disaster-Proof City Planner
    Create a master plan for a resilient urban zone. Use materials, infrastructure, and layouts that can handle floods, quakes, and hurricanes with style.
  • 🛤️ Future of Transportation
    Design a city-wide transit system that’s efficient, green, and innovative. Think electric buses, underground highways, or autonomous taxis.
  • 💧 Waterworks Wizard
    Build a water system for a city with drought problems. Combine recycling, desalination, smart meters, and more to keep the taps flowing.
  • 🌿 Green Building Design
    Create a low-impact building that’s a sustainability superhero. Use passive design, natural light, and eco-materials to shrink that carbon footprint.
  • 🏞️ Smart Infrastructure for Smart Cities
    Plan a system where IoT sensors and real-time data make traffic, energy, waste, and water systems smarter, and citizens happier.
  • ✈️ The Ultimate Airliner Design
    Design a futuristic passenger aircraft that reduces fuel use, boosts comfort, and lowers emissions. Focus on materials, shape, propulsion, and layout.
  • 🚀 Rocket to the Future
    Create a reusable rocket design for space missions. Include materials, engines, re-entry systems, and cost-efficient reusability features.
  • 🏁 Aerodynamic Drag Race
    Build a concept aircraft that slices through the air. Minimize drag, maximize speed, and explain your choices in wing shape, surface contour, and materials.
  • 🌍 Eco-Friendly Aircraft Engine
    Design a jet engine with a tiny carbon footprint. Think biofuels, electric thrust, or hybrid engines that still meet flight demands.
  • 💨 Jet Stream Exploration
    Create an aircraft that rides jet streams like highways. Include weather prediction systems, adaptive flight paths, and safety enhancements.
  • 🏗️ Supersonic Travel: The Next Frontier
    Design a quiet, eco-conscious supersonic passenger jet. Minimize sonic boom, reduce drag, and explain your propulsion tech.
  • 🌐 Autonomous Aircraft for the Future
    Build a UAV that navigates on its own. It must deliver packages, survey terrain, or patrol skies, all with smart obstacle avoidance and real-time decision-making.
