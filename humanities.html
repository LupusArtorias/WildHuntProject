Pick a Subfield and Begin the Hunt

From philosophy and language to ethics and the arts, unleash your critical thinking, creativity, and courage.

  • 🧠 The Ultimate Ethical Dilemma
    Create an original moral dilemma and solve it using a philosophical theory like utilitarianism, Kantian ethics, or virtue ethics. Explain why your approach is the most ethical.
  • 🧳 Philosophical Time Traveler
    Step into the mind of a historical philosopher and tackle a modern problem like AI or climate change. Speak their logic, use their method, and stay in character!
  • 💡 The Thought Experiment Challenge
    Design a fresh thought experiment to explore a deep question like free will, consciousness, or ethics. It should provoke debate and spark new insight.
  • 🏛️ Philosophical World Builder
    Construct a fictional society based entirely on one philosophy (e.g., existentialism or anarchism). Show how laws, behavior, and justice reflect the core ideas.
  • 🔍 Philosophical Investigative Journalist
    Critique a modern philosophical movement like postmodernism or feminist epistemology. Act as a journalist exposing its strengths, weaknesses, and cultural impact.
  • 🎓 Philosophy in Action
    Translate a complex philosophical idea into a tool for daily life. Build a practical guide that applies abstract theory to habits, relationships, or work.
  • 🧐 The Ultimate Metaphysical Debate
    Invent a new question about existence, the universe, or reality itself. Then explore and argue possible answers using old and new metaphysical ideas.
  • 📚 Literary Time Traveler
    Write a short story or poem placing a classic character like Hamlet or Gatsby into the modern world. How do they adapt? What themes still hold true?
  • ✨ Literary Mash-Up
    Combine two literary genres or authors (e.g., gothic horror and sci-fi, or Poe meets Austen). Create a story that blends styles and surprises readers.
  • 🖋️ The Author’s Workshop
    Invent a brand-new character for an existing literary universe like Hogwarts or Middle-earth. Describe their backstory, motivations, and how they fit in.
  • 🎭 The Unwritten Ending
    Rewrite the ending of a classic work like 1984 or Frankenstein. How does your version change the message or meaning of the original?
  • 📖 Literary Innovator
    Create a brand-new literary genre. Define its structure, style, rules, and write a short piece that shows how it works.
  • 🧳 Reimagining the Classics
    Adapt a literary classic into another medium, a screenplay, graphic novel, or musical. How does the medium change the interpretation?
  • 🎤 Literary Debate Club
    Build a philosophical or ethical argument based on a literary work. Use quotes and reasoning to defend your perspective, just like a true critic.
  • 🎨 Art Movement Reimagined
    Invent a new art movement inspired by historical styles (like Cubism or Impressionism) that addresses today’s issues. Write a manifesto and create a sample piece.
  • 🖼️ Artistic Time Travel
    Reimagine how a famous artist (like Van Gogh or Kahlo) might create work in today’s world. Combine their style with a modern setting or theme.
  • 🎭 Masterpiece Remix
    Take a famous artwork (like The Mona Lisa) and reinterpret it using a completely different medium — digital art, sculpture, even performance.
  • 📚 The Forgotten Artist
    Invent a fictional, overlooked historical artist. Describe their life, movement, style, and create a piece that represents their “lost” legacy.
  • 🏛️ The Art of Protest
    Create a piece of protest art that could have existed in a historical movement. Reflect its message and era using era-appropriate techniques and themes.
  • 🌍 Virtual Art Museum
    Design a digital exhibition that reinterprets an art period (like Renaissance or Art Nouveau) using AR, 3D models, or interactivity. Describe the visitor experience.
  • 🎨 Artistic Storyteller
    Write a story or create a visual sequence (comic, storyboard) inspired by a classical work of art. Tell a new tale through old masterpieces.
  • 🏰 Alternate History Architect
    Rewrite a major event like the fall of Rome or WWII. What if it ended differently? Describe the ripple effects across time and global politics.
  • 🛡️ Historical Documentary Creator
    Make a short video or multimedia project about a real historical event. Use primary sources and historical insight to bring it to life.
  • 🌍 The Global Diplomacy Summit
    Rewrite a major peace treaty (like Versailles). Negotiate borders, reparations, and security terms. Justify your changes with historical evidence.
  • 📜 The Time Traveler’s Guide
    Design a travel guide to Ancient Egypt or the Renaissance. What should visitors wear, expect, or avoid? Base your advice on real historical facts.
  • ⚖️ The Revolution Creator
    Invent a revolutionary movement in history, complete with leaders, strategy, slogans, and goals. Create a bold manifesto to spark social change.
  • 🏛️ Historical Fiction Writer
    Write a story from the perspective of someone history forgot, a street artist, soldier, inventor, and imagine how they lived through big events.
  • 🌟 The Historical Innovator
    Introduce a new invention into an earlier era. What if vaccines existed during the Black Death? Predict how history changes as a result.
  • 🧠 Create a New Language
    Invent a completely new language, including grammar, sounds, and sentence structure. Bonus: write a short story in it (with translation).
  • 📝 Dialects of the Future
    Design a futuristic dialect of an existing language. How has tech, environment, or culture shaped its evolution?
  • 🔠 Morphological Maze
    Create a puzzle where players break apart or build words using linguistic rules like prefixes, suffixes, and roots.
  • 🎙️ Phonetic Symphony
    Write a poem or song that follows unique sound patterns — tongue-twisters, alliteration, or phoneme challenges welcome!
  • 🌍 Linguistic Detective
    Design a code-breaking challenge where players decode an invented or unfamiliar language using linguistic analysis.
  • 🗣️ Cultural Linguistics Adventure
    Create a short skit, comic, or artifact that reflects the speech patterns and cultural quirks of a real-world language group.
  • 🔍 Syntax Scramble
    Build a sentence reordering game based on a custom syntax rule. Can players figure out your language’s word order logic?
  • 🎭 Theatre Remix
    Adapt a classic play like Hamlet or Oedipus Rex into a modern setting. Change the context, but keep the heart of the story alive. Present a rewritten scene and explain your creative choices.
  • 🎤 One-Person Show
    Write and perform a solo piece from the perspective of a historical or fictional character. Dive into their internal world and tell a pivotal story moment through monologue.
  • 🎬 Immersive Theatre Experience
    Design an interactive theatre piece where the audience becomes part of the story. Think guided tours, branching choices, or role-playing. Describe how your concept breaks the fourth wall.
  • 🎭 Performance Art Piece
    Create a non-traditional performance that tackles a current social issue like climate change or identity. Use movement, visuals, and emotion to convey a message without standard plot structure.
  • 🎭 Cinematic Theatre
    Write and stage a scene that blends live performance with cinematic techniques, use lighting, sound, and “editing” ideas to create a hybrid storytelling experience.
  • 🎤 Character Backstory Creation
    Take a known character (like Elphaba, the Joker, or Hamlet) and write a new monologue revealing untold parts of their past. Then perform it to showcase their emotional depth.
  • 🎬 Choreography and Emotion
    Choose a powerful emotion or theme and design a movement or dance piece that brings it to life. Present your choreography and explain the physical storytelling choices you made.
